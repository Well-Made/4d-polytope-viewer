4D Polytope Viewer

Interactive stereo viewer for 4D polytopes: the tesseract (hypercube) and the 5-cell (simplex), plus an ordinary cube to start with. Rotate them in all six 4D planes and see the depth as stereo pairs or with a wiggle. Source code and full documentation on GitHub.

LMB drag: rotate (XZ, YZ) · RMB drag: rotate through 4D (XW, YW)
RMB + mouse wheel: rotate ZW · LMB + RMB together, drag left/right: rotate XY
Wheel or pinch: zoom · Coloured axes in the lower-left corner of each image: fixed global axes (+X right, +Y up, +Z toward the viewer, +W toward the 4D observer)

How to look at it: relax your eyes so that the left and right image of a pair fuse into one 3D picture (parallel viewing; switch to cross-eyed under “Stereo pair” if you prefer that), or just switch the wiggle on and watch the motion parallax. The coloured edges show the position along the 4th dimension (blue far, red near).
